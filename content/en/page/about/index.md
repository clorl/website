French game designer, programmer and storyteller. I specialize in systems design with a focus on player 
agency and emergent gameplay. 

My goal is to provide players with "toy boxes" that empower them to solve 
problems in their own way. I take a hands-on, craftsman approach to my work, preferring to tinker in-engine 
and prove a design through experimentation before moving to production. To ensure systems are both balanced 
and intuitive, I integrate advanced mathematics, data analysis and UI/UX principles directly into my process. 

My constant curiosity for new technologies and workflows makes me a source of innovation and a versatile bridge 
between creative and technical teams. I don't hesitate to build my own scripts and tools to be more efficient.
